An interactive, high-energy, and cozy Roblox-themed date night web application built specifically for **Maddie** & **Sahil**.

## 🛷 The 5-Phase Mission Briefing

### 1. Phase 01: Avatar Matchmaker & Styling Station
- Interactive Avatar Generator with matching presets:
  - *Winter Sleigh Explorers* (Snow beanies, puffer jackets & scarves)
  - *The Classic Noob Duo* (Yellow skin, blue torso, green pants)
  - *Cozy Bedtime Pajama Squad* (Onesies & fuzzy slippers)
  - *Cyber Streetwear Rebels* (Neon hoodies & futuristic blocky shades)
- Synchronized Emote Dance checklist & spawn selfie photo-op.

### 2. Phase 02: "The Last Sleigh" Command Deck
- **Downhill Co-Op Adventure:** Navigate the snowbound slopes of Roblox's *The Last Sleigh*.
- **Integrated Canvas Sleigh Rush Mini-Game:** Playable arcade runner directly on the site with keyboard/touch controls.
- **Crash Penalty Roulette Wheel:** Interactive animated spinning wheel with custom date night dares and sweet penalties (fry feeds, shoulder massages, default dances, and kisses!).

### 3. Phase 03: YouTube Laugh Lounge & Snack Attack
- Curated categories for *Top Roblox Fails & Animations*, *Couple Gaming Chaos*, and *Wholesome Cozy Vibes*.
- Direct in-app theater mode modal with custom link/video ID launcher.

### 4. Phase 04: Anime Holodeck
- Interactive voting and decision dashboard featuring:
  - **Spy x Family** (Wholesome chaos, telepathic Anya, hilarious spy family comedy)
  - **Horimiya** (Heartwarming, cozy romance)
  - **Frieren: Beyond Journey's End** (Breathtaking winter magic and atmospheric animation)
  - **Delicious in Dungeon** (Monster cooking, dungeon fun & comfort food vibes)
- **Anime Roulette Wheel:** Randomized neural decision engine when you can't decide what to watch.

### 5. Phase 05: "Work At A Pizza Place" Feast & Cinema
- **Interactive Gourmet Menu Checklist:**
  - Double Cheese & Pepperoni Flatbread Pizza
  - Gourmet "Chezburger" Brioche Sliders
  - Crispy "Golden Block" Tater Tots & Fries
  - Chilled "Bloxy Cola" with citrus twist
  - Sleigh Hot Cocoa with square marshmallows
  - Chocolate Brick Brownies & Sundaes
- **Cinema Selector:** *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse*, *Suzume*, *Howl's Moving Castle*, *The Super Mario Bros. Movie*, and *Your Name*.
- **"Cinema Mode" Dimmer:** One-tap button that dims the entire webpage into an ambient movie theater glow.
